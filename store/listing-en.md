# English store listing (App Store en-US, Google Play en-US)

Since 1.53 the app has an English interface (picked by phone language, switchable in Settings → Appearance → Language).
Apple rule 2.3.10: the App Store texts don't mention Android or Google Play.

## Name (≤30)
```
SuperMind: Mind Maps & Tasks
```

## App Store subtitle (≤30)
```
Notes, habits and finance
```

## App Store promotional text (≤170)
```
Mind maps, tasks, notes, a planner and personal finance in one app — offline and ad-free. Your data stays on your device.
```

## App Store keywords (≤100, no spaces after commas)
```
mindmap,brainstorm,planner,to do,notes,calendar,habit tracker,pomodoro,budget,journal,xmind,outline
```

## Google Play short description (≤80)
```
Mind maps, tasks, notes, habits and budget in one app. Offline, no ads.
```

## Description (≤4000) — App Store and Google Play
```
SuperMind brings mind maps, tasks, notes and a personal planner together in one app. It works offline, with no ads and no subscriptions.

MIND MAPS
• 8 layouts: mind map, logic chart, org chart, tree, timeline, fishbone, brackets
• Themes, branch colors, relationships, boundaries, summaries, markers, images and notes in topics
• Outline and Gantt views, presentation mode, password-protected maps
• Import Xmind, Markdown and OPML; export to PNG, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Xmind

TASKS AND CALENDAR
• Lists, tags, priorities, subtasks and repeats of any kind
• Reminders with Done and Snooze buttons
• Kanban board, Eisenhower matrix, month, week and day calendar, export to your calendar
• Pomodoro and stopwatch with focus stats — the timer stays on your Lock Screen

NOTES — YOUR SECOND BRAIN
• Linked notes and a graph, daily note, templates
• Flashcards, question of the day, save articles from a link
• Voice dictation

PLANNER, HABITS, GOALS
• Habit tracker with streaks, mood, journal, goals with steps
• Steps and sleep from Apple Health check off your habits automatically

PERSONAL FINANCE
• Accounts, expenses and income, budgets, debts, subscriptions, analytics, exchange rates

WIDGETS AND SHORTCUTS
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for today's tasks and habits
• Quick actions from the app icon: new task, voice, focus, new map
• Share text, links and photos to SuperMind from any app

AI ASSISTANT (OPTIONAL)
• A map from an idea, breaking a task into steps, answers from your notes
• Free built-in AI or your own Claude key. The app asks before sending anything for the first time

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• Everything is stored on your device and works without internet
• Optional sync between phone, tablet, computer and browser through an account
• App lock with a passcode
• No ads, no trackers
```

For Google Play replace the "Steps and sleep from Apple Health…" line with:
```
• Steps and sleep from Health Connect check off your habits automatically
```
and "Lock Screen widgets" / "Share … from any app" lines stay as they are (Android has both).

## Note
Voice commands («завтра в 9 позвонить маме») still understand Russian only; English dictation of notes works through the phone's speech recognition.
